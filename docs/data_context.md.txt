# Data Context - DMart Capstone Project

## 1. Overview of Datasets
The project utilizes three practice datasets representing store operations, inventory status, and customer feedback across five regional DMart stores (Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Ahmedabad) over a 90-day period (2026-04-01 to 2026-06-29).

---

## 2. Dataset Specifications

### Dataset 1: `store_daily_clean.csv`
- **Description:** Cleaned daily operational metrics per store.
- **Row Count:** 450 rows (after removing 3 duplicate rows and ensuring clean daily records for 5 stores over 90 days).
- **Date Range:** 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-29.
- **Columns & Data Types:**
  - `date`: string/object (ISO format `YYYY-MM-DD`)
  - `store_id`: string/object (`S01` to `S05`)
  - `store`: string/object (City name)
  - `footfall`: int (Customer count, cleaned and imputed)
  - `revenue_inr`: float (Daily revenue in INR)
  - `daily_target_inr`: int/float (Target revenue)
  - `marketing_spend_inr`: int/float (Marketing expenditure)
  - `stockout_hours`: float (Stockout duration in hours)
  - `discount_pct`: float (Average discount percentage)
  - `revenue_per_footfall`: float (Feature: Revenue divided by footfall)
  - `marketing_efficiency`: float (Feature: Revenue divided by marketing spend)
  - `achievement_pct`: float (Feature: Revenue vs target percentage)
  - `shortfall_inr`: float (Feature: Target minus revenue)
  - `weekend_flag`: string (`Yes` for Saturday/Sunday, else `No`)
  - `performance_band`: string (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Poor`)
- **Data Quality Facts (Part A Guarantees):**
  - 3 exact duplicate rows removed.
  - 1 negative footfall anomaly (-150 in Hyderabad on 2026-04-21) set to missing and imputed.
  - 2 extreme high footfall outliers capped at upper IQR bounds (Mumbai: 6100 $\rightarrow$ 2986.00; Ahmedabad: 9800 $\rightarrow$ 2281.38).
  - 4 missing footfall values successfully imputed using store-specific medians.
- **Supported Questions:** Single-store daily lookups, date-range averages, target vs. actual achievement checks, multi-store revenue comparisons, and performance band tracking.

---

### Dataset 2: `inventory.csv`
- **Description:** Current stock levels and reorder thresholds across products and stores.
- **Row Count:** 40 rows (5 stores $\times$ 8 products).
- **Date Range:** None (Snapshot inventory data, no date column).
- **Columns & Data Types:**
  - `store_id`: string/object
  - `store`: string/object
  - `product_id`: string/object (`P01` to `P08`)
  - `product`: string/object (Product name and size, e.g., `Amul Milk 1L`)
  - `stock_units`: int (Current stock quantity)
  - `reorder_threshold`: int (Minimum stock threshold before reordering)
- **Data Quality Facts:** No missing values; Amul Milk (`P01`) specifically calibrated across stores.
- **Supported Questions:** Stock availability checks, identifying stores below reorder thresholds for specific products.

---

### Dataset 3: `feedback.csv`
- **Description:** Customer feedback ratings and categories.
- **Row Count:** 200 rows (40 feedback entries per store across 5 stores).
- **Date Range:** 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-29.
- **Columns & Data Types:**
  - `feedback_id`: string/object (`F001` to `F200`)
  - `store_id`: string/object
  - `store`: string/object
  - `date`: string/object (ISO format)
  - `rating`: int / missing (Customer rating from 1 to 5; contains 12 intentionally unrated rows)
  - `theme`: string/object (Feedback category: `Stock availability`, `Queue time`, `Pricing`, `Freshness`, `Staff behaviour`)
- **Data Quality Facts:** 12 unrated rows present; unrated rows are excluded when calculating average ratings.
- **Supported Questions:** Customer sentiment analysis, average store ratings over rated rows, most frequent customer complaint themes.

---

## 3. Relational Structure
- All three datasets are linked relationally through the **`store`** (or `store_id`) attribute, enabling cross-dataset insights (e.g., correlating customer feedback themes on stock availability with actual store stockout hours).
